# Month 12 — Capstone + Portfolio Polish

The final month has two jobs: ship one flagship, end-to-end capstone that's genuinely your best work, and go back over everything built in the previous 11 months to make the portfolio as a whole coherent and presentable.

## Focus

- One capstone project, in your highest-priority domain (Finance and Real Estate tend to have the broadest hiring relevance; Gold/Oil are strong if you're targeting commodities/macro roles specifically — see [Projects](../projects/index.md)), combining EDA, modeling, and — ideally — the deployment pattern from [Month 11](month-11-deployment-mlops.md).
- Portfolio-wide polish: consistent READMEs across all projects (goal → data → method → result), a coherent site/GitHub front door, dead links and stale claims fixed.
- A retrospective: what you'd do differently, which months you accelerated or slowed down, and what comes next after month 12.

## Resources

Everything in the [Resource Stack](../resources/index.md) and all prior months — this month is synthesis, not new material.

## Apply the V.A.D.A.++ loop this month

- **Visual (~15%):** one map of your entire 12-month journey — which months you sped through, which you slowed on, which projects taught you the most — genuinely useful for interview storytelling, not just a nice-to-have retrospective artifact.
- **Apply (~35%):** the capstone build itself.
- **Discuss (~25%):** do a full teach-back of the capstone as if presenting to a hiring panel — goal, data, method, what you tried that *didn't* work, result, what you'd do with more time.
- **Audio (~10%):** record that presentation-style walkthrough; re-listen and refine it before you need it in a real interview.
- **Calibration (~15%):** compare your Month 1 self-assessment of your skills (if you wrote one) against where you actually landed. This is the highest-value calibration check in the whole system — a full 12-month loop closed.

## Milestone deliverable

A flagship, end-to-end capstone project, plus a fully polished portfolio (consistent READMEs, working links, a finished site) and a public retrospective. This closes out the 4–8 project [hiring-ready portfolio](index.md#what-hiring-ready-actually-means-here) bar.
