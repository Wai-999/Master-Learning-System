Gold Price Forecasting

A causally-informed system for 10–15 day gold price forecasting

Three linked pages take you from raw data to a validated forecast: the end-to-end thinking flow with a curated reading list, a feature-by-feature causal explorer for this exact dataset, and an actionable system map you can step through in order.

This is a planning and reasoning framework, not a live trading model — it is meant to structure how you build and question your own forecasting pipeline.

Your dataset, at a glance

~70 columns across four families. Every page in this system refers back to these same groups.

Gold target & technicals

Gold_Close / Gold_Price, Gold_Open, Gold_High, Gold_Low, Gold_Volume, plus 10 derived indicators (daily_return, volatility, ma_7/30/90, rsi, macd, macd_signal, bollinger bands).

16 columns · target + autoregressive

Cross-asset markets

Full OHLCV for CPI_Proxy, DXY, SP500, Crude Oil, Silver, and Platinum — six instruments that carry monetary, inflation, growth, and precious-metal signal.

30 columns · 6 instruments

Macro & financial indicators

13 FRED series — rates (DFII10, DGS10, DGS2, FEDFUNDS, T10Y2Y), inflation (CPIAUCSL, T10YIE, M2SL), stress (VIXCLS, NFCI, ANFCI, STLFSI4), recession odds (RECPROUSM156N) — each with an *_is_filled flag.

26 columns · 13 series

Forecast objective

Predict Gold_Close / Gold_Price 10–15 trading days ahead, evaluated against naive baselines, with explicit uncertainty rather than a single confident number.

Horizon: 10–15 trading days
